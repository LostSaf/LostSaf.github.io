Download fallback

If Patient B fails to auto-download

Use the Mendeley “Download All” button, save the ZIP as imports/mendeley_97c3pf8h2h.zip, and restart ToothID. The launcher extracts the 14 tooth STL files automatically.

Why not hundreds of patients yet?

Licence and segmentation matter

Large arch-scan and CBCT repositories exist, but many are not individually segmented into redistribution-ready tooth meshes. This build only counts specimens that can be tied to a reliable source, tooth identity and reuse licence.
